The first public isme.gg release for Android phones, tablets and Android TV.

* Browse movies, series, anime and Filipino cinema. Filter discovery by streaming platform and return to the full catalog with All.
* On Android TV, use the remote to move between categories, preview focused posters and switch seasons without an extra Select press.
* Use portrait or landscape browsing on phones and open the native fullscreen player with seeking, subtitle and audio options when available.
* Resume titles from Continue watching. Check for updates or open the issue reporter from the app menu.

One signed APK supports Android 9 or later on ARM64 and ARMv7. Play media you have permission to access. Availability varies by source and region.
