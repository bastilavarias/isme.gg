## What's new

- My List on Android TV and smartphones, with locally stored bookmarks and watch progress.
- Redesigned mobile Search with cinematic category banners, genre browsing and recent searches.
- A dedicated Settings page for autoplay, screen-awake behavior, subtitles, availability region, storage and support.
- English and Filipino subtitle preferences, with adjustable subtitle size.
- Optional TV navigation sounds.

## Improvements and fixes

- Improved playback source discovery resilience.
- Fixed smartphone player Back navigation and TV bookmark activation.
- Improved settings alignment and remote navigation.
- Separate catalog cache and watch-history clearing without deleting saved titles.

## Installation

One signed Android APK supports compatible ARM smartphones and Android TV devices (Android 9 or later). Existing installations can update in place using the app's update flow. Bookmarks, watch progress and preferences stay on the device.
