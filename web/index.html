Frequently asked questions.

More about watching movies, series and Filipino cinema on Android and TV.

Where can I watch Filipino movies online?

Look for licensed services that offer Filipino films in your region. isme.gg is designed to help viewers discover Filipino cinema, including Tagalog movies, and play media they are authorized to access on Android and Android TV. Title availability depends on your source and region.

Can I watch movies, TV series, anime and K-drama on isme.gg?

isme.gg is designed for browsing these categories in one interface and watching compatible media you have permission to access.

Is isme.gg available for Android phones and smart TVs?

The app targets Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. The download section shows the current build and device requirements.

Can I watch Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video or HBO Max through isme.gg?

Platform filters help you discover titles available in your region. isme.gg operates independently of these services. Follow each provider’s subscription and access requirements.

Does isme.gg have ads?

The isme.gg interface is designed to be ad-free. Advertising or interruptions from external media sources are outside its control.

Is streaming with isme.gg anonymous?

The app stores watch history and preferences on your device. Peer-to-peer connections can expose your IP address to other peers.
