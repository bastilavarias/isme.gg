# Smart Next Episode — v1

- A single current playback session/decoder remains active. At 70% and after at least five seconds of stable forward playback, prepare next episode metadata and one memory-only candidate list. No speculative media bytes, second torrent session or additional disk cache in v1. The prepared list expires after 30 minutes and can be consumed by the same exact episode ID. If it is unavailable or fails, the existing player resolves normally. This does not guarantee instant playback.
- At 95% a heuristic Up Next card is shown (not verified credits detection). Only actual player completion starts the five-second countdown. Watch credits cancels the card/countdown; Play next is explicit immediate transition. Jumping to the end bypasses the 70% gate and uses normal loading as needed.
- Pauses, buffering, rewind below 60%, leaving the player and backgrounding cancel speculative preparation. Existing source network requests have bounded timeouts; cancellation may not abort a socket immediately. No automatic transition while the player is backgrounded or another route/dialog is active.
- Next episode follows provider numbering, crosses regular season boundaries, excludes known future air dates; missing dates are treated as listed episodes. Specials do not autoplay into regular seasons. Home menu has persistent autoplay toggle; off disables speculative prep and countdown, not manual Up Next.
- Tests: flutter test test/next_episode_test.dart. On Windows also run flutter analyze and full test suite. Verify normal threshold, scrubbing to 99% and completion, cancel/watch credits, pause/buffer/rewind, back and background, season change, specials, last episode, missing dates and errors. Check old controller disposal and no duplicate native session. Verify Continue Watching and ordinary movie playback. Test D-pad access to both overlay buttons, small displays and mobile.
- Full media-byte prebuffering remains a separate native-engine change: requires isolated bounded piece priorities, promotion without losing buffered data, low-memory/low-storage tests and measured bandwidth limits.
