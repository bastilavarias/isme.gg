# Mobile experience — TV feature parity

- TV is the product source of truth. The mobile presentation must expose the same destinations: Home, Search, Movies, TV Series, Anime, Pinoy, plus the menu. No standalone Browse or My List, bookmark action, copy-title action, or extra collection pages. Previously saved bookmark preferences are left untouched but no longer read or written by the UI.
- Portrait phones use a compact bottom bar for Home, Movies, TV Series, Anime and Pinoy, with Search and Menu in the app header. In landscape the same destinations move to a slim scrollable side rail. Search opens the native keyboard and the navigation bar/rail hides while it is visible. No destination is added or removed. Device classification remains based on Android UI mode.
- Home alone shows the provider rail. Movie, series, anime and Pinoy shelves follow the TV collection keys and order. Continue watching uses existing history and section filtering. Fixed-size poster shelves and touch-friendly details remain.
- Existing engine, native resolver, TV shell, D-pad, TV player and Android configuration are not modified by the parity correction. Mobile-specific touch player and episode list remain.
- The design refinement pass is separate: compact hero, artwork crop, spacing, typography, page transitions, touch targets, and landscape usability. Do not add features or destinations without approval.
- Verify on Windows: flutter analyze; flutter test; flutter test test/mobile_experience_test.dart. Check the exact six destinations, absence of Browse/My List and bookmark controls, provider rail only on Home, same collection keys, search, watch history, episode selection, orientation, playback and TV regressions. Check portrait on 360–430 logical pixel widths and landscape. No Flutter/Dart SDK on SEBASTECH; source checks are not device verification.
