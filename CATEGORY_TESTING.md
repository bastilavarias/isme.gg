# Dedicated catalogs — TV and mobile
No navigation redirects. Home, Movies, TV Series, Anime and Pinoy stay in one app shell, switch the active catalog and hero, reset vertical scroll, preserve local playback history, and reject stale asynchronous results from other tabs. TV uses separate nav hit areas and explicit remote Select/Enter handling; phone keeps bottom navigation and landscape rail. Shared section definitions live in lib/category_catalog.dart.

## Metadata behavior
- Movies: weekly trending, releases in preceding 90 days, action/adventure, comedy, horror/thriller, sci-fi, romance, highly rated, hidden gems (vote average >=7 with 30–500 votes), pre-2000 classics.
- Series: weekly trending filtered to non-anime series, premieres in preceding 90 days, returning shows with episodes near today's date, ended highly rated shows, crime/mystery, drama, comedy, Korean dramas, highly rated.
- Anime: weekly trending TV filtered to Japanese animation, airing, new premieres, action/adventure, fantasy/sci-fi, romance/slice of life via exact keywords, Japanese anime movies, highly rated favorites. Anime movies remain movies for playback, not episodic.
- Pinoy: Philippine-origin movies and series, recent releases, romance, comedy, action, horror, keyword-tagged independent film, pre-2000 classics. Popularity is not presented as weekly rank or app viewership.
- Weekly category ranks use up to two pages of actual trending TV data; display up to ten qualifying results. Never substitute unrelated popular content under a weekly rank. Some keyword-tagged rows may be sparse or unavailable. Highly Rated is community-rating-based, not a claim of critic reviews. Anime genre labels intentionally avoid claiming shonen/isekai without matching metadata.
- Existing encrypted device metadata credential enables expanded live sections. Without it, use matching Cinemeta and bundled regional collections; omit unsupported rows. Enable full catalog opens existing metadata setup, not an external page. No server-side catalog proxy, media cache, embedded access token, or analytics added.
- Read all available cache rows first; refresh missing/stale rows in pairs; native editorial cache bounded to 64 keys and 256 KiB per payload. TV keeps only active catalog shelves and horizontally lazy poster lists, with mounted vertical shelves for remote focus. Mobile uses slivers. Existing watch history is not migrated or cleared; new regional and anime-film tags are persisted, legacy history matched by known media ID where available.

## Validation checklist
- Run flutter analyze, flutter test (category_catalog_test.dart, home_sections_test.dart, mobile_experience_test.dart, cinema_shelf_test.dart and next_episode tests).
- Full Android rebuild needed (new native endpoint and queries); test on both TV and phone, portrait and landscape.
- Click every tab; use D-pad Left/Right, Select/Enter, Down to hero, then through every shelf, and Up to navbar. Test narrow TV screens, rapid tab changes while loading, selecting a title, back navigation, search and platform filters. Home should reset platform filter. Confirm each tab has distinct hero, visible loading, independently ordered collections and no redirects.
- Test configured/absent/invalid credential, offline cache, empty collection, refresh errors, and keyword lookup. Verify rankings, dates, genres, PH/KR origin, anime films and episodes against live metadata. Check that no fake ranks or mislabeled fallback appear. Verify old and new history and correct progress. No Flutter/Android SDK installed on SEBASTECH, so source checks are not actual compilation or runtime verification.
